# Forge — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Forge does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- It makes no network requests. All quotes and art are bundled inside the extension.
- It requests no browser permissions.
- It uses your browser's local storage for exactly two things: the shuffled order of quotes, so you don't see repeats, and the last few art pieces shown. This data never leaves your device, and removing the extension deletes it.
- There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/asadbektillayev7-byte/forge-newtab/issues
