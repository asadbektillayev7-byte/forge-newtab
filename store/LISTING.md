## Name
Forge — New Tab

## Summary (132 characters max)
A black new tab. One hard-hitting quote and one piece of animated line art. For the years that decide everything.

## Category
Chrome Web Store: Productivity / Firefox Add-ons: Tabs (or "Appearance")

## Description
Every new tab: pure black, one quote, one shape.

Forge replaces your new tab with a quote picked for guys 18–25: discipline, fear, failure, loneliness, feeling behind, purpose, time, money. There are 273 of them, from Marcus Aurelius, Musashi, Seneca and Dostoevsky to David Goggins, Jocko Willink, Alex Hormozi, Chris Williamson, Naval, Sam Altman and Elon Musk. Every attribution is checked, so there are no fake Einstein lines.

Above the quote, a piece of white line art draws itself in. There are 24 of them, and each one fits the quote it appears with:
• A grid of every day of this year, with the gone ones filled in
• 1.01³⁶⁵ vs 0.99³⁶⁵
• A boulder that slips twice and goes over on the third push
• A cracked square whose cracks become its strongest lines
• An hourglass, a lighthouse, a wall going up brick by brick, roots bigger than the tree, and more

You see every quote once before any of them repeat. Press N or → to skip.

No account. No tracking. No ads. No network requests. Nothing leaves your browser.

## Single purpose (Chrome Web Store asks for this)
Replaces the new tab page with a quote and an animated line drawing.

## Permissions justification
None. The extension requests no permissions. It only overrides the new tab page.

## Data usage (Chrome Web Store privacy tab)
Collects no user data. Tick nothing. Certify that data is not sold, not used for unrelated purposes, and not used for creditworthiness.
