# Why these quotes

The brief: quotes that a guy aged 18–25 needs to hear, ones that make him feel **seen**, not lectured.
This file records what the research found and how it shaped `src/js/quotes.js`.

## What young men actually say they're dealing with

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| **55%** of men 18–29 feel lonely at least some of the time. **43%** agree "I am inclined to think that I am a failure." | Institute for Family Studies / YouGov, 2025 (2,000 men 18–29) |
| **69%** of young men agree "no one cares if men are okay." Over half of men say "no one really knows me." | Equimundo, *State of American Men 2025* |
| Over a third of 18–24-year-olds feel no one will ever fall in love with them. | Equimundo, 2025 |
| 54% of men 18–29 say they're lonely, and most still want traditional goals (marriage, a house, kids) but feel they're out of reach. | YMRP survey, May 2025 (1,079 men 18–29) |
| Economic anxiety is the top worry, ahead of everything else. | Equimundo, 2025 |
| What young men lack is purpose: being told what to become, not just what not to be. The message they rarely hear is "we need you." | Richard Reeves (American Institute for Boys and Men), interviews 2025–26 |
| Comparison on social media ("their highlights vs. your bloopers") drives the feeling of being behind. | Talkspace and CNBC coverage of the quarter-life crisis |
| Stoicism took off with Gen Z men as a way to cope with pressure and uncertainty. The risk is a shallow, manosphere-flavoured version that keeps only "control your emotions." | Irish Times (2024), Trill Mag, Youth Press |
| *Vinland Saga*, *Vagabond* and *Berserk* resonate because they're redemption and self-improvement arcs. *Vinland Saga* in particular offers strength without cruelty ("I have no enemies"). | A Little Bit Human, Trill Mag, Reddit discussions |
| Gen Z's favourite motivational lines skew to Steve Jobs and David Goggins. | UK Gen Z motivation surveys, 2024–25 |

## The themes

Each section of `quotes.js` answers one of the findings above:

1. **Discipline: do the thing.** Habits, systems, showing up. It answers drift, dopamine loops and "I'll start Monday."
2. **Fear: go anyway.** Overthinking, and the gap between the man in your head and the one in the room.
3. **Falling, and getting back up.** For the 43% who think they're failures. Failure is framed as something normal and survivable, not as shame.
4. **Seen.** For the nights nobody asks if you're okay: Dostoevsky, Camus, Rilke, *Good Will Hunting*, *BoJack*, *Vinland Saga*. These aren't pep talks. They say "this is heavy, and you're not the only one carrying it."
5. **Behind? Compared to whom?** Against the highlight reel. Measure yourself against yesterday's you.
6. **Becoming a man worth being.** Purpose, responsibility, friends who sharpen you, family. Reeves's "we need you" in other people's words.
7. **Time.** Memento mori, without the doom. The *Days* art piece shows it literally: every day of the year, with the gone ones filled in.
8. **Body.** The iron, and a body you're not ashamed of.
9. **Work, money, building.** Answers the economic anxiety: skills, leverage, long games.

## Modern voices

Version 1.1 added the people this age group actually listens to now: Chris Williamson, Alex Hormozi, Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Jensen Huang, Jocko Willink, David Goggins, Naval Ravikant, Mark Manson, Ryan Holiday, Paul Graham, Jeff Bezos and Joe Rogan. Legends were added alongside them too: Napoleon, Churchill, Einstein, Goethe, Sun Tzu, Ali, Jordan, Kobe, Messi, Ronaldo and Pelé.

Wording was checked against the original source where one exists, for example Altman's essay [How To Be Successful](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful) and Hormozi's [Why I Work episode](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-game-with-alex-hormozi/why-i-work-ep-351). Williamson's "second best in the world" line comes from his [interview with Ali Abdaal](https://aliabdaal.com/podcast/chris-williamson/). Lines that only appear on quote aggregators with no source were left out.

## Curation rules

- **Real attribution or "Unknown".** A lot of famous quotes are misattributed, so several were fixed or cut:
  - "Be who you needed when you were younger" is Ayesha Siddiqi, not "unknown".
  - "Do one thing every day that scares you" is Mary Schmich, not Eleanor Roosevelt.
  - "The trouble is, you think you have time" is Jack Kornfield, not the Buddha.
  - "There is nothing noble in being superior to your fellow man…" is W. L. Sheldon, not Hemingway.
  - "Our greatest glory is not in never falling…" is Oliver Goldsmith, not Confucius.
  - "You can't go back and change the beginning…" is James R. Sherman, not C.S. Lewis.
  - "Discipline is choosing between what you want now and what you want most" is not Lincoln, so it's credited to Unknown.
  - Cut entirely: "Success is not final…" (not Churchill), "Find what you love and let it kill you" (not Bukowski), "Comparison is the thief of joy" (Roosevelt unverified).
- **No song lyrics** (copyright).
- **No bitterness.** Nothing anti-women, no "sigma" posturing. The tone is hard, but on your side.
- **Short beats long.** If a quote doesn't land in one read on a new tab, it's out.
